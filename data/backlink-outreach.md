## Notes

- **Best angle for all pitches:** AgentRank is *niche* (agents only, not all AI tools), *honest* (no sponsored rankings), and *deep* (85+ real reviews, not just listings). This differentiates from every general directory.
- **HackerNoon is free and self-serve** — highest ROI action. Write a compelling article and publish it this week.
- **Blog update pitches (RadiusTheme, SaaS Adviser, Project Aeon)** have the highest conversion rate because you're offering to improve their existing content.
- **Newsletter features** are harder to get but provide massive one-time traffic spikes. Worth the effort but don't depend on them.
- **Guest posts** build lasting backlinks but require more effort (writing the full article). Batch-write 2-3 articles and customize for each site.
